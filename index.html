Our story

Hearth & Bean started as a single roaster and six mismatched chairs. The idea was simple: source coffee we'd want to drink ourselves, and give the neighborhood a room worth lingering in.

Everything on the counter is made the same morning it's served — beans roasted in small batches, pastries from the oven two doors down, and a playlist that never gets louder than conversation.
